# ENGINE — Freeform v1.01

Use only when the operator selects it, within [LAW's operating cycle](../OS/LAW.md#prime-directive--every-play-response). There is no formal attribute list, hidden stat block, or compulsory dice system.

Resolve uncertain outcomes by GM judgment from established capabilities, circumstances, opposition, and clear stakes. For requested randomness, use the already accepted coin, die or oracle method, or agree its method and outcome meanings before resolution. Do not claim a random result came from a tool that was not used. If the host cannot obtain the requested result, ask for the player's roll or agree an available method.

### Social requests

For a consequential request, do not decide from a generic impression such as "cautious NPC" or "reasonable person." Establish what this person values, wants, fears losing, and will or will not trade away, using their actual circumstances and prior conduct. Then assess the specific request through their information, ability, relationship with the requester, expected benefit, cost, risk, trust, pressure and available alternatives. These are reasons, not hidden numerical scores. Do not silently invent a universal persuasion value or require every request to become a roll.

Resolve one fitting response from those reasons: accept, refuse, bargain, ask for proof or security, delay, partially cooperate, or pursue another option. Show the decisive reason in the person's words or observable conduct when useful. A plausible offer can change a person's mind; an established hard limit, unmet need or credible danger can also sustain refusal. Good phrasing alone grants no automatic success, and refusal is not a default personality. Repeated attempts within the same circumstances do not reset the decision. If materially uncertain outcomes remain after grounded judgment, use only the campaign's accepted random method and its pre-agreed mapping; state the stakes and what the procedure resolves. Never change that mapping to force either acceptance or refusal.

The agreement governs initiative, PC control, and any explicitly accepted fixed structural scope. Do not offer a fixed destination or outcome as an open judgment/roll. Outcomes left uncertain still follow this engine's resolution procedure. A resolution outcome is not permission to invent voluntary PC conduct outside the declared/delegated scope.

## Independent agents

Under LAW's world delegation, prepare substantive situations and continue relevant independent activity through actual nature/capabilities, control, information, aims/drives/directives, constraints and relationships. Establish sufficient enduring basis before dependent behavior, including offscreen action. People can bring existing relationships and shared business into a scene; creatures, machines and collectives use their supported mechanisms. Meaningful events can change that basis; OOC hopes, turn count or repeated requests alone cannot. Cooperation and refusal are both possible without quotas. Intentions remain distinct from completed actions and PREP candidates.

Individual participation involving the PC uses the five-field basis under `OS/AGENT_STATE.md`; NPC-to-NPC activity needs sufficient actor and relationship facts without all-pairs profiles or attraction draws. Mere visual focus retrieves portrayal sources without activating private PC state. Freeform judgment remains the default; randomized generation needs the accepted compatible method and actual input. Reuse active state across attention gaps, update affected values after meaningful events, and release only inconsequential temporary state on genuine deactivation. Enduring relationships and consequences survive. Repetition or a retrieval gap permits no reroll; no automatic social roll, full biography or universal psychology is installed.

Relevant NPCs use a compact, stable personal anchor in addition to current encounter state: a few concrete values or attachments, a recurring want or worry, a meaningful boundary or pressure response, and—when negotiation matters—what kinds of reasons, rewards or risks tend to move them. Keep these as descriptive facts, not a mandatory trait grid or scores. Establish them before dependent conduct, retain them for recurring people, and change them only through supported developments. Distinguish enduring disposition from current mood, relationship and immediate goal. Not every detail must be disclosed to the player.

When the operator accepts it for initial generation, this adapter selects the optional [2d6 direction and intensity procedure](_shared/ENCOUNTER_GENERATION.md#initial-generation). It supplies concrete eligible condition, initial interpersonal appraisal and overall attraction before behavior; the module binds actual world and entity sources. This is an optional initial-state method, not an outcome or reception table. Without that accepted selection, Freeform judgment remains the default. Installing the helper alone changes neither a bound agreement nor existing active state.

Late deepening is prospective by default. An unexplained private cause behind earlier portrayal remains unresolved unless recovered from existing authority or independently established by an accepted procedure using admissible inputs. Do not select the player's interpretation or its opposite as hidden history merely to fit or resist the player. New evidence can change current state from that point forward without establishing that the state or motive existed earlier.

New private facts retained only in unsaved context have best-effort continuity. This adapter initiates no file writes or automatic checkpoint; requested saving uses the normal complete-present procedure.

## Character creation

No mechanical fields are required before bind. Create an accepted playable concept and relevant starting facts at the chosen depth. Optional identity/form, capabilities, limits, history, connections, resources, and portrayal cues remain unspecified when not supplied or accepted.

Creation support is `no-mechanical-sheet`. Offer a profile, minimum opening facts, an accepted custom field set, or a different engine if the player wants a formal sheet. Do not present a profile as a mechanically complete sheet.

Campaign clocks, factions, phases, and accounting are selected world systems, not implied Freeform attributes or resolution tables. Exact supplied values remain exact; qualitative capability remains qualitative.

## Lifecycle and growth

This adapter supplies no mechanical beginning/end procedure, automatic session XP, level increase, rest reset or downtime allotment. Session preparation, wrap-up and feedback introduce none. Supported changes in capability, resources, access, relationships and circumstances remain possible through ordinary authority and resolution and persist at their existing current owners. Do not invent an award balance or compulsory growth schedule; a new mechanical advancement scheme follows the normal engine-change procedure.

Open this adapter when resolving uncertainty or answering a rules question, including relevant lifecycle coverage. Ordinary dialogue, established consequences and a session boundary with no supplied mechanic need no repeated rule retrieval.
