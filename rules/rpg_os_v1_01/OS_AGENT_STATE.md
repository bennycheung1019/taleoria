# Independent agents — enduring basis, relationships and participation

## Personal anchors and decisions (v1.01)

For a recurring or consequential NPC, retain a compact personal anchor that helps distinguish this person from the GM's generic judgment: what they value or are attached to, what they are trying to get or protect, a meaningful boundary or pressure response, and (where relevant) what kinds of incentives, assurances or risks can move them. Describe only a few connected facts. Examples include valuing coin over reputation, loyalty to a sibling despite distrust of their cause, or keeping promises to regular customers while refusing unsecured debt. Do not require every possible trait, a balanced virtue/flaw pair, numeric loyalty/greed scores, or a secretly sympathetic explanation. These are established personal facts, not a checklist to display or repeat. Keep them in the enduring person record when available; the five encounter fields remain temporary current state.

When the NPC considers a request, resolve the actual request for this person, not an abstract persuasion test: what they understand; whether they can do it; what they gain, risk, spend or compromise; who they trust or feel responsible to; what alternatives and pressure they see; and which established priorities or boundaries apply. Determine which reasons genuinely matter to this NPC before deciding. A request may yield acceptance, refusal, a counteroffer, conditions, delay or partial help. Show the main reason through dialogue or observable conduct where appropriate, without exposing private state the PC cannot know. A distinct anchor must create distinct plausible responses; it does not guarantee a particular answer or make one personality type automatically persuadable. Do not add hidden numeric values or invent a persuasion score. Use the bound engine's judgment; for remaining consequential uncertainty use an accepted random method only when applicable and with its meaning fixed before the result.

### Proactive participation

An NPC who is present and has an active concern can initiate conversation or action when the situation gives them a natural opening. In travel, waiting, shared work, meals, aftermath and other stretches of meaningful time, check whether a participant has something they want to ask, disclose, propose, contest, negotiate or do. Let their own goal, relationship, new information, discomfort or response to events supply the reason. They may interrupt, change the subject, speak to another NPC, or choose silence. Do not wait for the player to prompt every NPC turn, and do not force a line, conflict, revelation or event when nothing in their situation motivates it. Portray initiative without narrating the PC's reply, feelings, consent, attention choice or next action. Preserve active state across pauses; initiative does not reset an unresolved decision.

Cold support for LAW's **Understand → Establish → Resolve → Portray** cycle. This procedure owns sufficient actor establishment, group conduct, the five-field PC encounter baseline and its lifecycle. Open for an unresolved question and reuse loaded guidance; no startup or per-dialogue read is required. Enduring circumstances and relationships give actors continuity beyond PC contact; temporary encounter state describes their current participation, not their whole existence. This procedure supplies no additional file-write grant.

## Identify the actual decision

During [Understand](LAW.md#understand), identify the declared attempt and the surrounding situation: who has relevant business, which relationships or processes matter, and where actual decisions belong. Selecting an opportunity, establishing an actor and resolving an encounter are different tasks. A meeting does not settle a negotiation; the player's hoped-for result establishes none of its causes.

## Recover sufficient authority

During [Establish](LAW.md#establish), recover relevant current individual/system state, then materially needed stable sources. Follow direct pointers; use [RETRIEVAL](RETRIEVAL.md) when the route is unclear. Individual facts outrank group tendencies; hard constraints and established exceptions retain their scope.

The Setting Brief supports ordinary background. Retrieve the narrow nature, species, culture, design, faction or control source before supplying focused detail or adjudication governed by it. When the module prescribes a first-focused-portrayal guide, obtain its applicable guidance before the first focused physical description or closer observation; broad brief coverage alone does not satisfy that trigger. Reuse sufficient retrieved authority. MODULE supplies setting facts and source bindings, ENGINE owns reusable generation mechanics and their resolution boundary, and INSTANCE owns later current state; appearance alone establishes no custom or capacity.

Distinguish enduring private truth, active temporary state, legitimately discarded incidental state, PC knowledge, deliberately unfixed answers, eligible unauthored detail, inapplicable fields and missing required sources. An unread required record is not an eligible gap. Not applicable is not unknown or zero. Preserve exact promises, disagreements and qualifications when material.

## Nature and control

Before dependent behavior, including the first individually relevant encounter or consequential offscreen action, recover or establish the actor's nature, capabilities, control and sufficient enduring personal or entity-appropriate basis. Intelligence, knowledge and mechanical capacities retain their source/engine authority; a state adjective supplies no unrecorded skill, statistic or ability.

For every individually relevant person, alone or in a group, establish something they value, want, enjoy, protect or worry about, grounded in concrete personal circumstances, attachments or dispositions. Give that basis practical implications for choices, attention, boundaries or initiative before using it. A few connected sentences can suffice; occupation, current business, generic virtues and attitude toward the PC alone do not supply it. Recover existing facts and author eligible gaps under the accepted grant; leaving authorable substance unspecified is not a default. Scale depth to the actual interaction without preparing biographies for a background crowd.

Allow affection, pride, pleasure and hope alongside envy, prejudice, selfishness, grief and conflicting loyalties where appropriate. No person requires a prescribed balance, secret, trauma or dramatic conflict, and an unpleasant attitude need not conceal a sympathetic explanation. Private circumstances need not be disclosed or outwardly signaled; present happiness can coexist with private pain. For other actors use their actual drives, learned associations, directives or mechanisms without imposing human psychology. The existing temporary encounter fields use this basis and do not replace it. [Optional examples](../ADMIN/CAMPAIGN_BUILD.md#compact-personal-basis) illustrate the scope without adding fields or generation rolls.

Locate the actual decision owner and any local discretion. People, creatures, machines and collectives may act through discretion, instinct, programming, orders or shared control. Organizational membership does not erase individual discretion; a controlled body need not be a separate mind. Shared control does not imply shared knowledge: use established channels, scope and delays. A change of control or lost link requires its supported transition.

Shared governing state stays with its controller/system owner. Reference it once and keep only relevant local differences with the individual. Each entity's sensing, classification, control and response use its own supported mechanisms. Evidence for a capacity establishes only what it supports; failure to observe a capacity does not establish its absence. Retrieve or establish the necessary compatible basis before dependent resolution; do not fill unknown capacities with a human or monster stereotype.

## Initial Encounter Baseline

At activation involving the PC, recover surviving facts and complete all five current fields before the entity's individual attention, appraisal, initiative or response. A clause per field often suffices. Establish the basis before outward behavior, not as its retrospective explanation. "Initial" means this participation episode, not the beginning of the actor's existence or a permanent impression.

Reuse the five values throughout participation and change only those affected by substantive circumstances. Continuing dialogue needs no new determination or restatement. Keep material establishments and changes through the selected persistence procedure; do not repeat unchanged profiles or reorganize records on disengagement. ADMIN checkpoint/save compiles canonical owners.

| Field | Required determination |
|---|---|
| Condition and mode | Present physical, emotional or operational condition and immediate activity or mode, with causes only where established. |
| Priorities and constraints | What it currently pursues, preserves or avoids; its own needs, commitments, orders and practical limits, with concrete implications for the encounter. |
| Perception and appraisal | Whether and how it detects the PC, what information it receives or recognizes, and its current interpretation, which may be mistaken. |
| Attraction | Where applicable, its present overall attraction to or aversion toward the PC within the supported attraction domain. Physical/aesthetic, sexual and romantic meaning can be resolved later when relevant; the initial value must not wait merely because those subtypes are unneeded. |
| Engagement stance | Its current openness and relevant boundaries, or appropriate nonsocial response policy. Derive it from the established combination and situation; it informs resolution of the particular request without preselecting its outcome. |

Each field must have a concrete established value, a supported capacity-based inapplicability, or a precise source/perception limitation with the condition that ends it. Unknown is not neutral or zero. Being busy, on duty, partnered, ordinary or inconvenient to develop does not make applicable attraction irrelevant. Absence of perception delays only unsupported PC-specific determinations; first sufficient detection makes them due before their first use. Missing required sources must be recovered; eligible unauthored details use the accepted method rather than remaining blank by convenience. A defined absence of present attraction is a value, not a missing entry. Supported exclusions retain their actual scope; an exclusion of sexual attraction does not itself exclude aesthetic appreciation. Attraction and aversion are distinct from trust, general approval, hostility, availability, commitment and participation. Predation or a chemical gradient is not interpersonal attraction by analogy.

Use accepted world and individual facts, actual control, pre-encounter circumstances and the authorized authoring method. Causal history involving the PC is valid input; an unperceived player wish, unverified interpretation or desired success/refusal is not. Give a priority concrete implications for the current encounter, independently of the PC's desired outcome. Include supported positive aims and constraints without inventing a refusal requirement. Neither cooperation nor refusal proves independence. No universal personality or attraction table is installed by this OS procedure.

### Scope and scale

Individual coverage is triggered before the entity individually participates with the PC: its own attention or appraisal, interaction, initiative, or a personal decision resolved separately from coordinated group conduct. A particular NPC looking at the PC can be such participation even before either speaks. A bare approach can activate an encounter without a declared PC purpose; NPC initiative can activate one without the player's selection. Merely describing or closely observing an unaware entity does not activate that entity. First focused physical portrayal still retrieves any prescribed portrayal guide independently of activation. Establishment itself grants neither initiative nor NPC awareness and adds no PC action or inner response.

Coordinated conduct uses genuinely shared orders, conditions and information without a personal baseline for every member. Separate decisions involving the PC require separate baselines; shared location or activity is not shared decision ownership. There is no numerical or on-duty exemption. Background crowds need no roster.

For a social group, establish relevant relationships and ongoing business before portraying their effects. Who trusts, enjoys, resents, desires or relies on whom, what is unresolved, and what each is doing can matter before the PC joins. Establish each relevant tie's direction, knowledge and expectations; feelings need not be mutual or known to their subject, and a person's interpretation need not be true. Shared location does not establish prior acquaintance. Carry exchanges among participants through changes of speaker and attention: an arrival, interruption, disagreement, shared joke or departure follows their actual circumstances, not a scene quota. NPC-to-NPC interaction and offscreen decisions need sufficient actor, relationship and situation facts, not PC-directed five-field profiles or all-pairs appraisal/attraction draws. Complete a PC baseline only when that participant actually engages the PC under the trigger above.

Preserve existing individual differences even during group treatment. A collective's true controller may have one shared baseline with local body differences and actual information channels. A group of independently deciding entities is not thereby one mind; its operational stance assigns no member's personality or attraction. When a member individually participates, inherit only applicable shared facts and surviving personal state, then determine eligible current gaps before response. Previously unmodelled private feelings remain unestablished at the earlier group sighting, not retrospectively fixed or absent. An active individual is never reset into an anonymous group to evade continuity.

## Active participation and deactivation

Keep the baseline while the entity remains a participant in the local encounter or an unresolved causal exchange. A lull, shift of attention, glance away or neighboring conversation does not deactivate it. Ongoing relevant interaction, a joint task or unresolved causal involvement retains the participant through pauses. Mere co-location, occupation or remaining in a particular venue neither activates an entity nor keeps it active. Nearby background entities are not automatically active. Do not maintain a whole-building, city or population roster.

Deactivate when participation has reasonably ended and no unresolved involvement requires that temporary state. A completed exchange with no continuing involvement can end the active episode; a completed local task can release its participant when nothing unresolved requires that temporary state. A momentary exit, repeated request, scene label or attempted retry cannot end an ongoing negotiation, pursuit, danger or other unresolved exchange. Physical absence alone is insufficient when the entity remains causally involved. Account for operative consequences before release, without inventing offscreen activity.

Discard only inconsequential private temporary values on deactivation. Keep material identity, personal circumstances, relationships among actors, promises, injuries, knowledge and unresolved intentions at their proper owners. Consequential trust, attraction or conflict survives when established as durable; a later mood draw cannot erase it. An alarm or fault survives its instigator's departure. Enduring importance does not keep an otherwise uninvolved actor's whole temporary profile active forever; audit disclosure alone creates no such importance.

On a later incidental encounter, fresh eligible temporary state is authorized within surviving facts. Previously discarded private impressions, attraction or mood need not be recovered or reproduced. This is a new present determination, never a replacement account of past behavior or permission to erase a known commitment. Historical transcripts remain evidence of their time; they do not reactivate discarded values as current authority. A missing still-active value or consequential fact is not legitimate expiry and must use the recovery procedure below.

## Establish eligible gaps

Author eligible new facts within the accepted scope, including private facts the PC has not learned. No separate permission is needed for each authorized secret. Keep deliberately unfixed answers open until their trigger; recover missing authority rather than replacing it. A small basis does not require a biography or settle every later outcome.

Determine fairness-sensitive facts before resolving the attempt they affect. Use the applicable ENGINE or accepted compatible method. World creation binds an actual usable default generation procedure and necessary opening exceptions, with source routes, eligible dimensions, constraints, dice meanings and actual input method; a 'use baselines' flag is insufficient. Fix admissible inputs, scope, constraints and outcome meanings before generating. Existing facts and hard exclusions come first. Coherent conditional generation is allowed; independence from a desired outcome does not require statistically independent traits. An unfamiliar entity needs a sourced or authorized compatible profile before dependent behavior, not an assumed human default. A new method needs acceptance where not already authorized; conflicting rules changes follow the engine-change procedure.

Claim randomness only from actual randomizer or player-supplied input. Otherwise use authorized judgment or agree an alternative. Retain the actual method and result at its needed scope; a seed alone needs its algorithm, inputs and mapping. A biased question or table remains biased when rolled. An inconvenient result does not authorize a new table, reinterpretation or reroll. Repeated contact within active participation reuses its state; genuine later incidental activation follows the release rule above. Seeking another entity requires actual time and opportunity.

## Resolve operative opportunities

During [Resolve](LAW.md#resolve), use the established basis, actual developments, available channels and applicable ENGINE procedure. Prose traits supply no automatic numerical modifiers. Initial-fact generation may establish an impression or attraction only within its accepted scope; it cannot decide the pending offer's acceptance, reciprocity or consent by relabeling that result an initial impression. Resolve a reception once from the baseline under the bound engine, without a duplicate establishment or substitute willingness roll.

At relevant scene entry, meaningful accepted elapsed time or changed information/conditions, consider implicated independent activity as well as due open matters. Actors can organize, converse, work, seek help or change their relationships before the PC asks. Under delegated world authorship establish eligible new circumstances and their sufficient basis before resolving them; sparse records do not mean everyone waits. Check actual opportunity, information, means and constraints, then resolve uncertainty under the engine. An intention is neither guaranteed completion nor an indefinitely frozen task. Keep the actual offscreen outcome separate from PC knowledge. Unrelated actors need no check, scheduled simulation or universal activity roll.

Bound the question and interval. Retain an outcome at its resolved scope; repeated queries or subdivisions create no extra draws. Stop at the first applicable interruption and leave later time uncommitted. Retain a useful question/window, method and result with the existing state. Only an actual operative condition needs an Active processes cue.

## Fallback oracle for eligible unknowns

Use when requested or accepted as a standing method. Facts and applicable ENGINE procedures take priority; grounded judgment remains sufficient where supported. The fallback resolves eligible uncertainty still open after those; it does not replace a missing source, regenerate fixed state, open a deliberately unfixed answer early or create an unsupported capacity.

1. Frame one concrete yes/no question and its scope before drawing. Exclude impossible outcomes and preserve fixed facts and PC authorship.
2. Use the accepted oracle, or this convention: one actual d6, **1–3 No; 4–6 Yes**. These odds are a game convention. Standing acceptance needs no per-roll reconfirmation. If real randomness is unavailable, obtain the player's roll or agree an alternative.
3. Retain the question, mapping, actual input and scoped result. Resolve its consequences through actual circumstances and rules. Do not invent prior motives or history to justify it.

An eligible outcome's importance does not exempt it from the accepted method. An omitted resolution remains an audit gap until accepted correction; retrospective dice or reasons cannot fill it.

## Portray and change

During [Portray](LAW.md#portray), express the resolved situation through supported conduct and perceptible detail. Keep private state, outward presentation, the PC's declared interpretation and the entity's own supported interpretation distinct. Observed speech and actions constrain facts without proving their perceived meaning. An entity uses its own information; unperceived OOC wishes and others' secrets do not enter its knowledge.

Express conversational aims and relationships through particular speech, response and meaningful body language. Participants can address one another while including the PC; choosing another speaker does not suspend everyone else's business. Their interests and available information determine which wider developments enter talk, conduct and routines. Where supported by the world, traditions, gathering places and belonging coexist with rivalry, mistrust, prejudice or exclusion, without compulsory incidents, trauma or a balance quota. Reveal enough perceptible context to act without explaining every hidden motive. Courtesy can coexist with dislike, and cooperation can follow duty. Attraction, trust, cooperation, commitment and voluntary participation remain distinct. Preserve PC authorship; ordinary declared conduct needs no extra permission ritual.

Update affected active fields from actual developments, received information and elapsed activity. A completed task becomes history; a change of topic alone supplies no fresh baseline draw. Neither variation nor a desired dramatic beat erases constraints. Quiet exchanges, solitary actors and routine success remain valid, with detail proportionate to meaningful decisions and the player's chosen attention.

## Deepen prospectively, not retrospectively

Deepen during preparation or play when useful to the impending situation, before the new facts affect behavior or outcomes. A private cause not established before its observed behavior remains unresolved. Recover an earlier cause from pre-existing authority or independently determine it through an accepted procedure whose inputs exclude the player's interpretation, desired outcome and the need to explain old portrayal.

Earlier observations constrain eligible answers without choosing their hidden meaning. Do not select either the player's interpretation or its opposite to accommodate or resist it. Leave an unnecessary unresolved cause open. New evidence may change current beliefs, aims, orders or control; it does not rewrite them backward. A tired appearance alone does not establish an earlier long shift, and later behavior alone does not establish an earlier control failure.

When timing matters to continuity or audit, retain a compact source/method or establishment cue with the factual state. A later claim that a fact was already decided is not independent proof. Retain facts and provenance, not internal reasoning.

## Retain, save and transfer honestly

`INSTANCE/NOW.md` owns one **Active encounter state** block for temporary baselines at an authorized save. Membership means active: no extra yes/no flag, inactive registry or permanent record for every contact. Retain the five current values, precise limitations and compact method/result references needed for active continuity. A concise entry can use identity or an existing person pointer followed by the five clauses; a shared controller is referenced once with relevant local differences. A shared operational group entry does not replace separately required personal state.

PEOPLE owns enduring individual facts and relationships, including consequential NPC-to-NPC developments; NOW or the existing system owner retains shared situations and processes. Give a shared fact one authority and references rather than conflicting copies. Established private circumstances belong there, not solely in revisable PREP candidates. Do not duplicate enduring facts in the temporary block. CURRENT_SAVE carries the necessary resume pointer. Preserve active and unresolved state across saving/handover; omit expired profiles at the next authorized write. Deactivation itself requires no deletion tool or file write and erases no historical evidence.

This procedure grants no private dossier write, background save or establishment checkpoint. The selected [write-only play log](../ADMIN/PLAY_PERSISTENCE.md) retains material new establishments, including consequential undisclosed facts and intentions, as well as changes; unchanged profiles need no repetition. Otherwise new state remains in working conversation until authorized persistence. Notes are fallible recovery material, not independently verified hidden memory. Preserve actual facts and inputs without regenerating missing values. Consolidation follows [CLOSE_CONTRACT](../ADMIN/CLOSE_CONTRACT.md); a checkpoint retains its archive boundary. Handover follows [SCENE_HANDOVER](../ADMIN/SCENE_HANDOVER.md) with source authorities frozen until authorized reconciliation.

Preserve established entities, relationships and history when adopting this guidance. Recover required current state and fill only eligible gaps; accepted release does not erase a consequential fact. Use [CORRECT](../ADMIN/CORRECT.md) for disputed facts; changed authoring methods operate prospectively through their accepted procedure. Relabeling an established consequence as an incidental impression is not a repair.

If still-active or consequential state is unavailable, seek its source, preserve supported outcomes and identify the narrow gap. Pause only decisions dependent on it. Saving, review and resumption supply neither missing causes nor automatic reroll authority. Established genuine deactivation, rather than failed retrieval, authorizes later incidental regeneration. Do not recover expired inconsequential values merely to recreate a hidden dossier.
